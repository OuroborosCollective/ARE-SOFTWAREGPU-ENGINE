# November 2026 research: structural GLB evidence (offline only)

Source: Rushikesh Zawar and Vladimir Kim (Adobe), **Is This 3D Asset Usable? Beyond the Beauty Render for Asset Curation**, arXiv preprint v1, 2026-11-03: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04102

## What the paper actually measured

The authors' Diagnostic Rendered Evidence (DRE) pipeline uses aligned beauty, wireframe and UV-seam-plus-wireframe *render passes*, frozen ViT classifiers and late fusion. The paper reports **86.36% balanced accuracy and 88.32% bad-asset recall** over 1,234 annotated TexVerse assets; an external 764-asset set reports 88.87% balanced accuracy. These are **authors' results and not independently reproduced by ARE**. Their dataset labels, learned weights and visual score are not imported by this change.

## What ARE actually implements

A **read-only source-inspection prerequisite** that emits SHA-256-pinned structural evidence from embedded GLB 2.0 BIN data. It does not emit beauty/wireframe images, material shading or classification predictions.

- Triangle index-topology: used vertices, edges, boundary and nonmanifold edges, connected components, Euler characteristic and exactly collinear/zero-area triangles. Index topology is **not** a watertightness or printability conclusion.
- Candidate UV seam positions: coincident float32 **POSITION** coordinates with differing float32 **TEXCOORD_0** coordinates. Duplicate-vertex UV seams are only a heuristic; no seam coverage, UV island correctness or texture-quality rating is claimed.
- Material/texture-resolution provenance: texture references are validated for internal indices, but external URIs are **not opened**. An external URI reports `external_unresolved` rather than guessing the image, its appearance or its license.
- Input budget: at most 128 MiB per GLB, 8 MiB JSON and 200,000 indexed triangles per primitive; large/unsupported, sparse, compressed, integer/normalized or unindexed layouts produce explicit `not_analyzed`, not fabricated geometry.
- Deterministic JSON output with `source.sha256`, `authority:offline-derived`, `verdict:not_assessed` and each of the three DRE render passes `not_executed`.

Run on a **real** existing GLB (local, no network):
`python -m tools.glb_diagnostic_evidence path/to/asset.glb --output diagnostic.json`

Smaller optional per-primitive analysis budget:
`python -m tools.glb_diagnostic_evidence path/to/asset.glb --max-triangles 10000`

Test:
`python -m unittest software_gpu.tests.test_glb_diagnostic_evidence -v`

A small valid test GLB is generated by the test runner with an actual BIN chunk; it is **not a production/source asset**. Wolfram independent graph-count check for indexed triangles:
- Open 4-vertex square, 2 triangles: (vertices, edges, faces, components, boundary edges, Euler) = (4,5,2,1,4,1)
- Two disconnected triangles: (6,6,2,2,6,2)

No glTF-validator compatibility certification, no 3D render proof, no CPU-FPS speedup, no production import or training/ML reproduction is claimed. Require unchanged standard CPU/Mesa/LLVM and packaging CI on the final PR head; preserve `Memory.md` append-only before merge. Compressed/sparse/extension-defined geometry needs separately supported importers and integration regressions.

## Additional genuinely new research assessed but not transplanted

- **AbacusGS** (arXiv:2611.01534v1, 2026-11-01) reports **4.5x end-to-end OpenGL splat-frame speedup** on a CPU-sorted *Gaussian Splatting* workload, at approximately 0.13 dB lower PSNR vs a fine-tuned control. The method explicitly changes ordering, is not a byte-identical triangle raster path, and code was announced but not yet released in the paper. Do **not** apply group reordering to ARE's deterministic triangle submission/depth contract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01534
- **VecRec** (arXiv:2611.02451v1, 2026-11-02) compiles semiring recurrences into vectorized C++ kernels, with up-to-an-order-of-magnitude kernel-level speedup in its authors' examples. Re-association / FMA / float32 algebra can alter actual raster sample results, so no direct ARE edge-step rewriting is authorized without differential RGBA8/depth proof: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02451
- **Rasterization-based discontinuous integration on the GPU** (arXiv:2611.05186v1, 2026-11-03): investigates OpenGL/CUDA area-integration accuracy and MSAA at discontinuities, not CPU full-frame raster FPS. Its coverage-boundary methodology is relevant for future focused negative tests, not a drop-in backend: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05186

All four items are **preprints or workshop research**, not an independently replicated new ARE runtime performance result.
